About

Touring Video • Live Streaming • Concert Films

1,000+ Productions
125+ Artists & Clients
2015 Est. Nashville, TN

Emphasis Films is a Nashville-based mobile production company with broadcast-quality multi-camera systems and fly packs ready to deploy worldwide. Over more than a decade, Emphasis has delivered upwards of a thousand productions—touring video, live streams, and concert films—for over 125 artists, venues, and brands.

The name is older than the company. It started in a high school band practice, with one of our founders stopping rehearsal to give the lead singer this note: you're singing monotone—put more emphasis on the words. The band didn't last. The note did. A thousand productions later, the job hasn't changed: make sure nothing about a performance lands flat.
